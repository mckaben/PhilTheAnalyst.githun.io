Economic Indicators & Income Analysis

Research Question & Overview

Investigates the structural relationship between median household income ($77.7K baseline), labor force participation (98.06%), and poverty rates (13.01%) across Colorado jurisdictions to identify vulnerable economic sectors.

Dataset & Source

U.S. Census Bureau and American Community Survey (ACS) 5-Year Estimates.

Transformation, Modeling & DAX

Transformed raw tables in Power Query, connected county geography shapefiles, and implemented custom DAX formulas for income-to-poverty ratios and income growth rate metrics (36.86%).

Key Insights & Recommendations

  • High Latino concentration areas exhibiting poverty rates >25% face structural wage gaps despite labor participation rates over 90%.
  • Recommendation: Direct vocational initiatives toward counties like Pueblo and Alamosa to bridge median wage gaps.
